Modern websites often overload attention before users even start reading

Many interfaces compete for attention with banners, badges, sidebars, and inconsistent contrast. For users who already experience visual strain, colour confusion, migraines, or cognitive fatigue, this can turn ordinary browsing into unnecessary work.

ClearView is a lightweight browser concept that helps reduce clutter, improve contrast, and support a steadier reading flow. Instead of redesigning the whole web, it adapts pages at the moment a user needs relief.

Demo goal: Show how a user can switch from a distracting layout to a cleaner, more predictable reading experience in one click.

This prototype focuses on three simple interventions: hide nonessential visual noise, improve readability through spacing and type scale, and offer higher-contrast presentation for dense content. These changes are modest, but together they can meaningfully improve usability.

The best accessibility tools do not force people to change their workflow. They remove friction while keeping the original task intact.
